HERITAGE & MISSION

About the Association

Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, JSPM University Campus, Pune.

🎯

Our Mission

To bridge theoretical classroom curriculum with industry reality by equipping students with comprehensive, hands-on laboratory experience across microcontrollers, sensor engineering, and PCB prototyping.

🚀

Our Vision

To be a premier collegiate forum where engineering curiosity evolves into patented innovations, competitive hardware hackathons, and socially conscious digital technologies.

🤝

Institutional Culture

We champion collaborative integrity, academic camaraderie, mentorship from experienced senior researchers, and vibrant campus celebrations bridging technical rigor and creative expression.
