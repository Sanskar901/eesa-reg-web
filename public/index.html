About EESA Committee

Nurturing technical brilliance and leadership in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between classroom curriculum and industry demands by providing practical, hands-on hardware, embedded systems, and robotics experience to every engineering student.

🚀

Our Vision

To cultivate a vibrant community of innovative thinkers, researchers, and tech leaders ready to tackle real-world global challenges across smart hardware and digital intelligence.

🤝

Holistic Culture

EESA is not just about circuits and code. We celebrate camaraderie, cultural diversity, sports tournaments, and student mentorship throughout the academic year.

INNOVATION IN ACTION

Our Works & Key Initiatives

Explore the flagship technical, industrial, and social projects spearheaded by EESA.

WORKSHOP Annual Initiative

Hands-on ESP32 & IoT Microcontroller Bootcamps

Intensive training modules where students design real-time sensor networks, MQTT cloud gateways, and smartphone-controlled IoT appliances with complete hardware kits.

👥 250+ Students Trained 📦 50+ Projects Built
INDUSTRY EXPOSURE Study Tours

Industrial Visits to Electronics & Auto Majors

On-site industrial tours to leading manufacturing plants including Tata AutoComp, Bharat Electronics, and semiconductor fabrication units in the Pune tech corridor.

🏭 5+ Plants Visited 🎓 Direct Engineer Mentorship
COMPETITION National Level

Circuitron: Hardware & Circuit Hackathon

A grueling 24-hour national challenge where inter-college teams design, simulate, and etch functional analog and digital circuits to solve critical industrial problems.

🏆 ₹25,000 Cash Pool ⚡ 40+ Competing Teams
SOCIAL IMPACT Community Outreach

E-Waste Awareness & Safe Recycling Drives

Campus-wide collection campaigns gathering discarded circuit boards, batteries, and appliances for certified scientific disposal and component re-purposing for student laboratories.

♻️ 300+ kg Recycled 🌱 Green Campus Certified
